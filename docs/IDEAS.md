# Ideas to evaluate

## Waste and colony maintenance

The [current refuse loop](../README.md#nest-refuse) links larval feeding to
temporary brood-place pressure. Test it with an occasional breeder, a colony
running at full brood throughput, and a player returning to a full buffer.
Look for a meaningful choice between extra buffer space, carrier staffing,
and smaller brood batches. A full buffer should be recoverable by reassigning
workers while existing brood keeps developing.

Other potential waste sources, such as carcasses, and separate contamination
mechanics are open ideas. Leaf-cutting ants offer a model for isolating refuse
in chambers or outside piles. [Waste management study](https://repository.si.edu/bitstream/handle/10088/1389/Bot_Currie_Hart_and_Boomsma.pdf)

## Other ant behaviors

- Temporary food sources could reward keeping replacement workers ready.
- Worker-to-soldier specialization could trade ordinary labor for stronger
  protection during territorial contests.

## Development

- Measure complete founding paths with real-engine scenarios and playtests.
  A compact next-goal indicator is worth revisiting if players get lost.
- Extract large UI sections gradually as they change. Share live requirements
  and effect display across related actions, and leave gameplay rules in the engine.
- Plan save migration before public players depend on long-lived colonies.
