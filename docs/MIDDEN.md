# Refuse Midden

After Nursery level 2, the colony may excavate a Refuse Midden. Building uses
sugar and tunnelling space and opens five **Refuse Carrier** jobs. Standard
larvae add one refuse per second each. Each carrier removes three per second.
The backlog caps at 100. With a carrier assigned and at most 20 refuse,
ordinary development receives 20% more care. An overflowing or unstaffed
midden returns care to its usual speed. Food costs per brood do not change.
The feature is optional and resets on founding.
